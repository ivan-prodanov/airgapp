How it works

A small box in your car.
An app on your phone.

  1. 1

    The box keeps your car's internet in check.

    Your Tesla goes online through the box, never on its own. Maps and music get through. Telemetry and camera clips stop at the box.

  2. 2

    Your phone talks to your car through the box.

    Tap Unlock in Airgapp, from the couch or from another country. The box passes it on, and your car does it.

The app

You already know this app.

It works just like Tesla's, screen for screen, so there's nothing new to learn. The difference: it reaches your car through the box, so it keeps working while your car is gated.
